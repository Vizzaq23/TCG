Mamaragan, Perona and Electrical Luna were also checked on the live TCGplayer English Near Mint price panels; those three drafts use the live values. Other values are product/printing market benchmarks, not condition-specific quotes.

Singles are used for cards worth $2 or more. Lower-priced SRs and rares, plus C/UC worth at least $0.25, use four-card playsets. Target 95% of market rounded up to $0.05, with $2.49 single and $2.99 playset floors for the existing free-shipping setup. These floors can exceed card-only market value.

Cheaper C/UC go into 50-card lots by set, provisionally $9.99 with duplicates disclosed. The lot price is a proposed offer, not a TCGplayer lot valuation. Do not put valuable C/UC into these lots.

## Before activating

1. Count copies by printed card number and exact art. A playset needs four copies; available playsets equal floor(copies allocated / 4).
2. Add counted singles/playsets to the owner collection and link each draft to that exact collection row. Allocate each physical card once. Bulk lot stock counts completed 50-card lots, not individual cards.
3. Record the acquisition cost, packed postage, supplies, and time per order. Review current prices before activating.
4. Confirm usable payment checkout in the deployed shop. This work did not change payment configuration.

Profit is not established until costs are supplied. Stripe standard US domestic-card processing is [2.9% + $0.30](https://stripe.com/pricing); actual account/payment-method fees may differ. Net proceeds = order revenue - processing - postage - packing - card costs - labour - other applicable costs. At a $2.99 item price and that processing rate, about $2.60 remains before all other costs.

Six card numbers with multiple ordinary printing-price rows are held for printing review: OP01-005, OP01-068, OP01-086, OP02-040, OP02-041, OP03-115. Special arts, promos, leaders, SECs and extra-booster-numbered cards were excluded from this bulk draft batch.
